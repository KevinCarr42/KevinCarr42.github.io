AI/ML Pilot Project — Lead Developer

AI Translation Pipeline

An AI-powered translation system fine-tuned on official CSAS documents to deliver faster, more accurate, and cost-effective English-to-French translations of scientific reports. Led this proof-of-concept pilot project through multiple phases: data gathering and transformation, model fine-tuning, rules-based preferential translations, translation quality evaluation, and integrated system development.

The project demonstrated that AI translations are often superior to published translations, with significant improvements in consistency for scientific terminology and substantial reduction in translation time and costs.
